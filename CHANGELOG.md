- Service card hover treatment (`docs/MOTION_REDESIGN.md` §5 item 1, built to the bolder direction rather than the restrained micro-interaction the doc originally specified — §2 amended to match). An angular brand-blue wipe sweeps across the card on the `ease-confident` curve, the content inverts to near-black, and a burst of shards radiates from the bottom-right corner, echoing the hero's shard field at micro scale. No runtime JS — it's CSS state on `:hover`/`:focus-visible`, so it adds nothing to INP. The ticker (`Marquee`) is deliberately unchanged; §5 notes it's already correct and cheap as CSS-only.
  - The wipe is an animated `clip-path` polygon on a stationary panel, not a translating skewed panel. That's what makes the inversion safe: because the panel never moves, an inverted copy of the content sits inside it permanently pixel-aligned with the real content, so blue is only ever revealed together with the near-black text that belongs on it. The obvious first approach — translate a panel and transition the real text's `color` — leaves a ~250ms window each hover where light text sits on a half-arrived blue field.
  - brand-500 + neutral-950 is the pairing the primary CTA already uses (7.4:1) and holds in both themes, so the inverted copy needs no light/dark branching. The card border stays on `--accent` rather than brand-500: in light mode a brand-500 edge on white is only 2.55:1, under WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 for identifying a component, while `--accent` (brand-800 there) keeps a 5.25:1 boundary around the blue fill.
  - The burst is a short wide band pinned to the card's bottom edge, not a square corner cluster, and that's a correctness constraint rather than a style choice: the card is `flex-col justify-between` with the arrow last, so the summary's final line always clears the bottom edge by at least `p-6 (24) + h-5 (20) + mt-6 (24) = 68px`, and more when a card stretches to match its grid row. A 64px-tall burst box therefore sits inside that gap by construction at every breakpoint and summary length. Earlier corner placements were positioned by eye and collided with copy as soon as the card width changed the wrap points. Verified across viewports by measuring each card's burst-box top against its summary's bottom (4px floor; 26.8px actual, since the shards don't fill their box).
  - The inverted duplicate renders with `div`s rather than `h3`/`p`, so the document keeps exactly one heading per card for anything reading the DOM outline rather than the accessibility tree; `aria-hidden` on its wrapper is what excludes it from the a11y tree. Verified 6 cards / 6 `h3`s on the homepage. Note that the browser tooling used here builds its tree from the DOM and ignores `aria-hidden` (confirmed by control test), so the a11y-tree exclusion rests on the attribute being correct per spec, not on a tool-verified tree.
